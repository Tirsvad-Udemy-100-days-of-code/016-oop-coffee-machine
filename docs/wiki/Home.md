# OOP Coffee Machine

An object-oriented console coffee machine: order a drink, pay with US coins and get your coffee, a refund or change.

## What is it?

Day 16 of the Udemy course *100 Days of Code: The Complete Python Pro Bootcamp*. The coffee machine of day 15 is rebuilt with classes. A `Menu` lists the drinks and finds one by name, a `CoffeeMaker` holds the water, milk and coffee and makes the drink, and a `MoneyMachine` takes the coins, gives change and keeps the money earned. The machine serves espresso, latte and cappuccino. Type `report` to see the resources and the money in the machine, and `off` to switch it off.

## What the exercise teaches

- Modelling a program with classes that each have one job: Menu, CoffeeMaker and MoneyMachine
- Keeping the assignment's class and method names
- Storing the menu, coins and starting resources in constants
- Writing automated pytest tests, and running a linter and a strict type checker

## What it does

- Espresso, latte and cappuccino with their own ingredients and prices
- A check of water, milk and coffee before a drink is served
- Payment with quarters, dimes, nickels and pennies, with a refund or change
- `report` shows the resources and the money earned; `off` switches the machine off
